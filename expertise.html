Expertise

Regulatory & Policy Leadership

As President of the American Spray Drone Coalition — representing roughly 80% of the U.S. agricultural drone market — I engage the FAA, FCC, and Department of Commerce directly on the rules shaping this industry's future.

  • Authored ASDC's comments on the FAA's Part 108 BVLOS Notice of Proposed Rulemaking, addressing right-of-way safety rules, electronic conspicuity requirements, and conflicts with EPA field-safety regulations that BVLOS operations create in practice.
  • Filed comments with the FCC on proposed restrictions on foreign-made drones, proposing a structured transition — including a legacy spare-parts window and tariff-revenue reinvestment into domestic manufacturing — that protects national security goals without stranding the farmers and operators who depend on this equipment today.
  • Has submitted related comments to the Department of Commerce's Bureau of Industry and Security on UAS trade and national-security rulemakings.

Industry Intelligence

Launched the industry's first Impact Survey in 2024 and expanded it in 2025, establishing the benchmark data now cited by NAAA and agricultural trade publications: 16.4 million acres treated, up 58.7% year-over-year, across 1,710 Part 137 operators.

Where the Market Is Actually Headed

The commercial drone market isn't a few years away — it's already here, at six and seven figures a year, at companies like Verizon, Walmart, and Duke Energy. The strategic question isn't how to out-build DJI on hardware. It's who builds the interoperable layer — mission execution, data, compliance — that lets manufacturers compete on aircraft while operators get integration and choice.
